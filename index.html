Case study · May – July 2026

A tactical report on the 2026 World Cup

Andrea Marinozzi, football journalist at DAZN, was preparing a pre-tournament series on YouTube. I designed the analysis behind it and built the website that hosts it, covering every one of the 48 qualified nations.

  • Built a structured dataset from scratch from qualification matches: possession, PPDA, passing accuracy, touches in the box, big chances.
  • Developed the full website: group pages, radar charts, head-to-head comparisons and tactical boards with formations.
  • Supervised a team across data collection, tactical writing and editorial review to deliver publication-ready reports.
